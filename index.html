Serendipity hosts guests in three British colonial bungalows across Ooty, Devala and Coonoor. I designed the website that introduces the houses, sells the experiences around them and gets people to check availability.

The brief

Make a hill-station weekend feel one click away.

  • 01
    Introduce three houses at once

    Lymond House in Ooty, Amberina in Devala and 180 McIver in Coonoor each need their own identity without splitting the brand.

  • 02
    Put booking in reach from the first screen

    Property, dates, guests and rooms sit in one bar directly under the hero.

  • 03
    Sell more than a room

    Film shoots, weddings and celebrations, corporate retreats, and nature walks with tea-estate tours.

  • 04
    Answer the questions before they are asked

    Accommodation types, families, events and how to get there, next to a hand-drawn map of the hills.

This reading of the brief comes from the structure of the design files.

Structure

Six pages, two jobs

The home and properties pages sell the idea of a stay. The three property pages and About Us close the deal with detail.

Design moves

Heritage on paper, booking in brass

Cream paper texture, pencil sketches of the bungalows behind the content and serif small caps carry the colonial feel. Brass is reserved for the things you can act on.

01

Booking before scrolling

The availability bar carries every field a stay needs: property, check-in, check-out, guests over 12, guests under 12 and rooms. It ends in one brass "Check availability" button.

6 fieldsOne rowAbove the fold
02

Three houses, one family

Each property gets its own wordmark and photo, but the same numbered card: place in small caps, house name in italic, then a one-line pitch.
